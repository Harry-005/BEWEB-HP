Galvanised Beads That Protect and Align Plaster Corners for a Crisp, Straight Finish

Product Overview

What Is GI Corner Beading?

GI Corner Beading is a galvanised iron angle bead fixed to external wall corners before plastering. It acts as a straight guide for the plasterer's trowel, keeping the corner line true, and then protects that edge from knocks and chipping once the wall is finished.

The galvanised coating stops the bead rusting and staining the plaster over time, so the corner stays clean and sharp for the life of the wall.
