Alkali-Resistant Fibre Mesh That Reinforces Plaster and Prevents Cracking

Product Overview

What Is Fibre Plaster Mesh?

Fibre Plaster Mesh is an alkali-resistant fibre mesh embedded within the plaster coat on interior and exterior walls. It reinforces the plaster layer, holding it together as the surface cures and as the building experiences minor movement.

By spreading stress evenly across the wall surface, the mesh significantly reduces the fine surface cracking that plain plaster is prone to, giving a smoother, longer-lasting finish coat.
