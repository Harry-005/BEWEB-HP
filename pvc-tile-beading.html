Product Overview

What Is PVC Tile Beading?

PVC Tile Beading is a moulded trim fitted along exposed tile edges — at steps, counters, wall corners and open ends — to finish the edge cleanly and protect it from chipping and impact damage.

Made from tough, weather-stable PVC, our beading is supplied in standard profile depths to match common tile thicknesses, and can also be manufactured to a customer's own colour, length, profile and packaging requirements.

Material PVC
Profiles Available 6mm to 12mm (Customisable)
Primary Benefit Chip-Free, Finished Tile Edges
Typical Use Tile Edges, Steps, Counters, Wall Corners
