Our Story

More Than Just A Juice Bar.

River Juice Bar was born in Poonamallee out of a passion for pure, unadulterated fruit nutrition. We believe healthy choices should taste extraordinary.

River Juice Bar started with a simple vision by founder Hariharan: to bring high quality, 100% natural cold-pressed juices and smoothies to the Poonamallee & Chennai community.
