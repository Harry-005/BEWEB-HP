Product Overview

What Are PVC End Caps?

PVC End Caps are moulded protective covers fitted over the exposed ends of reinforcement bars on site. Pushed securely onto the rebar tip, they cap off the sharp end and shield it until the bar is embedded in concrete or the structure is finished.

Made from tough, weather-resistant PVC, our end caps stay in place through handling, formwork and site movement, giving contractors a simple, reliable way to protect workers and keep exposed steel from injury hazards and surface corrosion until it's cast in.
