Frequently Asked Questions

Have Questions? We’ve Got Answers

What products does Balaji Enterprises manufacture?

We manufacture and supply PVC Cover Blocks, Tile Spacers, Rebar Caps, PVC End Caps, GI & Fibre Plastering Mesh, GI Chicken Mesh, GI Corner Beads and other PVC construction accessories. See our Products page for the full range.

Do you deliver Pan-India?

Yes. We dispatch orders across India with dependable schedules that keep site work on track, in addition to serving contractors and builders locally in Chennai.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities vary by product and size. Share your requirement through the form above or call us directly and we’ll confirm the best quantity and pricing for your project.

Can I get GST-compliant billing?

Yes, we are GST registered and provide fully compliant, transparent billing for every order, large or small.

How can I request a quote or product brochure?

Use the enquiry form on this page, call or WhatsApp us using the buttons above, or visit any product page for a downloadable brochure and technical specification sheet.

Do you offer samples before a bulk order?

For larger project orders, samples can usually be arranged. Mention this in your enquiry and our team will guide you through the process.
