Product Overview

What Are PVC Tile Spacers?

PVC Tile Spacers are small, precisely moulded inserts placed between tiles during laying to hold a consistent gap for grouting. Positioned at the corners or edges of each tile, they keep rows straight and joints uniform across the entire floor or wall before the tiles set.

Made from rigid PVC, our tile spacers hold their shape under the weight and movement of tiling work, giving installers a fast, reliable way to achieve a professional, evenly-gapped finish without constant manual measuring.

Material Rigid PVC
Sizes Available 2mm to 10mm
Primary Benefit Uniform Tile Gap & Alignment
Typical Use Floor Tiles, Wall Tiles, Bathrooms, Kitchens
