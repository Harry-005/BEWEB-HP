Product Overview

What Are PVC Cover Blocks?

PVC Cover Blocks are precision-moulded spacers placed between reinforcement steel and the shuttering face before concreting. They hold rebar at a fixed, accurate distance from the formwork so that the specified concrete cover is maintained consistently across columns, beams, slabs, footings, rafts and walls.

Manufactured from durable PVC, our cover blocks replace traditional mortar or stone blocks, giving site teams a lightweight, non-corroding, dimensionally accurate alternative that installs faster and performs reliably for the life of the structure.

Material Virgin & Reprocessed PVC
Shapes Available Round & Square
Primary Benefit Accurate, Consistent Concrete Cover
Typical Use Columns, Beams, Slabs, Footings, Rafts, Walls
